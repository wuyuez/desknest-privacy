DeskNest

Privacy Policy

A little company for your desk. Use an iPhone on its own or connect a compatible DeskNest screen.

Updated November 13, 2026 · DeskNest 1.1.0

Scope and operator

This policy covers the DeskNest iPhone app provided by Fuding Xiaxiansheng Technology Co., Ltd. Use it to display content locally, set up another iPhone or connect a compatible DeskNest screen. No account is required. There are no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information on your devices

Layouts, component titles, dates, focus state, backgrounds, manually selected weather cities, device names and connection information are stored on the relevant devices. The system photo picker gives DeskNest only the photos you select for cropping, compression and display. It does not grant full-library access or change your originals. Pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Local transfers

The display device you choose receives the corresponding settings and photos. iPhone-to-iPhone sessions use Apple MultipeerConnectivity with required encryption. Compatible screens use authenticated local HTTP, not TLS encryption; use a trusted local network. Content is not relayed through developer servers. Cloud remote control is not offered.

Wi-Fi and firmware

During setup or Wi-Fi switching, the network name and password you enter are sent locally to the selected screen, which stores them to join the network. The app does not persist the entered Wi-Fi password or send it to the operator. Firmware updates send your selected firmware file to the screen. Local update-recovery records do not include pairing tokens.

Weather requests

Weather is optional and currently supports cities in China only. You choose a city manually; the app does not request system location permission. Search terms and city codes are sent directly to weather.com.cn over HTTPS. The service can see the request and network IP and handles its own service logs under its rules. Requests do not include photos, pairing credentials, advertising identifiers or a user account. There is no developer weather proxy. Weather snapshots are cached locally on the iPhone or screen.

Collection, tracking and this website

The operator does not collect photos, display settings, pairing credentials, Wi-Fi passwords or usage analytics through the app. The app does not use advertising identifiers, sell personal information or track you across apps. It has no public sharing community. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process basic access logs under its own rules. The pages contain no analytics scripts.

Storage, deletion and choices

Data stays on the relevant device until you change, delete or clear it. Deleting the app normally removes ordinary local files on that iPhone; it does not remotely erase copies on other display devices. Keychain credentials may remain under system behavior. Removing a device entry does not erase that physical device. Recropping copies or old photo caches may remain after a photo is removed; delete the app for a full local-file reset, and follow screen instructions to clear the accessory. You can revoke Local Network permission in iOS Settings. Basic clocks do not require photos, weather or an accessory.

Children, contact and updates

DeskNest is not designed specifically for children and does not publicly publish user content. For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact support@xiafresh.com with your device model, iOS/app version and a description. Do not send private photos, Wi-Fi passwords or pairing credentials. We update this page and date if processing changes and provide additional notice when needed.
